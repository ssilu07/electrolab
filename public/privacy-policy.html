ElectroLab Privacy Policy

Application: ElectroLab (ElectroCraft Physics & Circuit Lab) • Package: com.royals.electolab • Effective Date: October 2026

Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

ElectroLab is strictly designed with privacy-first architecture. We do NOT collect, transmit, profile, or sell any personal data, emails, contact lists, or background location information.

1. Introduction & Scope

ElectroLab is an educational interactive electronics and circuit simulation application developed for students, engineers, and educators. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency regarding your privacy when using our mobile and web applications.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because our entire physics and circuit solver runs directly in your local environment, we do not require or collect:

  • Personal Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
  • Location Data: No GPS or network location coordinates.
  • Device Hardware IDs: No IMEI, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers.
  • Analytics & Trackers: No marketing trackers, behavioral tracking, or cookies.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Vibration / Haptics

Used solely to deliver physical haptic feedback when pressing toggles and during circuit overloads.

Audio Synthesis

Uses Web Audio API to procedurally synthesize clicks, buzzers, and tones. No microphone access is requested.

Local Storage

Circuits, sandbox components, and settings are preserved strictly on your local device.

Canvas Snapshot

Allows you to export high-definition schematic images directly to your device storage.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

ElectroLab is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations. The app contains no user-generated public chat forums, no in-app purchases, no advertisements, and collects zero personal data, making it completely safe for school and home use.

5. Third-Party Services & Google Sites

The application may be downloaded through the Google Play Store and references documentation hosted on Google Sites. Google Play Store services may collect standard anonymized crash diagnostics in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy.
