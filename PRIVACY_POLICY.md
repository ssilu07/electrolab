## 1. Introduction
ElectroLab is an educational interactive electronics and circuit simulation application developed for students, engineers, makers, and educators. We place the highest priority on user privacy. 

This Privacy Policy explains how ElectroLab handles information and affirms our commitment to a **Zero-Personal-Data-Collection** architecture.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
ElectroLab is built to run entirely on your local device. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, profile, or sell any of the following:

- **Personal Information:** No real names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or user account registrations.
- **Biometric or Demographic Data:** No age, gender, facial recognition, or fingerprint data.
- **Location Data:** No precise or approximate GPS/network location information.
- **Device Identifiers:** No IMEI numbers, advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), MAC addresses, or hardware serials.
- **Contacts, Media & Files:** We do not read your contacts, photo albums, camera feed, or local documents.
- **Tracking & Analytics:** No advertising trackers, behavior tracking beacons, or marketing profiling cookies.

---

## 3. Permissions & Device Features Used
ElectroLab only uses minimal device permissions essential for the operation of the circuit simulator:

| Permission / Feature | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Provides tactile haptic feedback when turning switches on/off, adjusting potentiometers, and signaling circuit overloads/short circuits. |
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required by the modern web runtime wrapper (Capacitor/Chromium WebView) to load local application assets and open external help links if clicked by the user. No personal data is sent across the network. |
| **Web Audio API** | Synthesizes procedural sound waves locally (e.g., relay clicks, buzzer frequencies, 555 timer oscillators). The app **never** accesses the device microphone or records any sound. |
| **Local Storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage)** | Saves your sandbox circuits, saved schematics, and challenge completion status strictly on your physical device. |
| **Canvas Snapshot & Sharing** | Generates visual circuit schematic images locally when you choose to export or share your creations. Data is encoded directly into shareable hashes or exported image files under your direct control. |

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
ElectroLab is designed for educational use and complies fully with:
- The **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**
- The **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K)**

Because ElectroLab does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). It contains no chat rooms, no unmoderated community features, and no in-app purchases.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** ElectroLab does not display third-party banner ads, interstitial ads, or reward video ads.
- **App Distribution:** When downloaded through the Google Play Store, Google may collect anonymized system diagnostics or crash statistics in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 6. Data Security & Storage Management
All user-created circuits, custom presets, and level progressions are stored solely within the application's local sandbox storage on your device.

- You retain complete ownership and control of your circuit designs.
- You can permanently delete all stored data at any time by clearing the app data through Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > ElectroLab > Storage > Clear Data`) or by using the in-app "Clear Circuit" button.
